---
title: Guestbooks for sub-collections
---
Guestbooks allow you to collect data about who is downloading the files from your datasets. You can decide to collect account information (username, given name & last name, affiliation, etc.) as well as create custom questions (e.g., What do you plan to use this data for?). You are also able to download the data collected from the enabled guestbooks as CSV files to store and use outside of DataPublication.

If you enabled guestbooks for your sub-collection, the dataset contributors to your sub collection can select a guestbook by clicking on *Terms* →  *Edit Terms Requirements*.

How do you create a guestbook?

1.  After navigating to your sub collection, click on the *Edit Dataverse* button and select *Dataset Guestbooks*.

2.  To create a new guestbook, click the *Create Dataset Guestbook* button on the right side of the page.

3.  Name the guestbook, determine the account information that you would like to be required (all account information fields show when someone downloads a file), and then add custom questions and make them required, if desired.

    ![guestbook-2](uploads/93d80caf8dca5b2fad73cf3d0612b350/guestbook-2.png){width=1000 height=456}

4.  Click on the *Create Dataset Guestbook* button once you have finished.

5.  After creating a guestbook, you will notice there are several options available in the list of guestbooks: From the *Manage Dataset Guestbooks* page, there are options to (from left to right) disable, view, copy, edit a guestbook, download responses, delete a guestbook and view responses.

![guestbook-4](uploads/84f9acd4bd7b0740e44cd84fe2f99511/guestbook-4.png){width=900 height=211}

**Hint:**

  - By default, guestbooks inherited from the parent Dataverse collection will appear. If you do not want to use or see those guestbooks, uncheck the checkbox that says *Include Guestbooks from \[Parent\]*.

**Please note:** Guestbooks are currently disabled in the DataPublication Dataverse collection. Sub collection administrators can however independently create guestbooks for their sub collections.
