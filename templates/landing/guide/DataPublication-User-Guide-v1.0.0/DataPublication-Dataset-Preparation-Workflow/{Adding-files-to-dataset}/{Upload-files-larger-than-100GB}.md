---
title: Upload of files larger than 100 GB
---
Unfortunately, it is not possible in DataPublication to handle files larger than 100 GB, as this is the current limit for individual file size. However, you can split your dataset into smaller files or chunks of up to 100 GB to upload larger datasets:

- You have the flexibility to structure your dataset as needed, with each file being under 100 GB. If parts of the dataset can be used independently, it makes sense to store them in separate files. On the other hand, if the dataset only makes sense as a whole, it may be better to archive everything into one file and split it into sub-archives.

- Sub-archives can be as large as 100 GB, though we recommend 20 GB files for easier downloading, as described in the "Download" section of this guide XXXXXXX.

- We suggest using the free 7z software for compression, which works across all platforms and creates highly efficient archives. You can download 7z [here](https://7-zip.de/download.html).

- The simplest option, however, is to upload your data as-is, as outlined in the [previous section](/DataPublication-User-Guide-v1.0.0/DataPublication-Dataset-Preparation-Workflow/%7BAdding-files-to-dataset%7D/%7BUpload-files-between10-100GB%7D), and let our team handle the rest. We will split and archive your dataset as needed, based on your preferences and in direct consultation with you.

Once you have files that are no larger than 100 GB, continue with the steps described previously.
