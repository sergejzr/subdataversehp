---
title: Independent sub collections
---
If you wish to set up an independent sub collection within DataPublication, where all institutional or project-relevant datasets can be created and published, please contact our team.

The possibility of creating a collection within DataPublication is attractive in particular for larger projects and units, eg. CRCs, clusters of Excellence, institutes and departments that wish to keep their published research data in one place.

As the entity will be responsible for curating their collection, it is required they have personal ressources available (eg. data manager, data steward, project coordinator) to allocate for a sub collection administrator.

**Hint:** If you would be interested in setting up a sub collection for your project, please contact the DataPublication team for further assistance: <<DP-SUPPORT-MAIL>>

Every entity interested in setting up an independent sub collection has to sign an agreement. For further details on independent sub collections or receiving access to the proper agreement form, please contact the DataPublication team: <<DP-SUPPORT-MAIL>>
