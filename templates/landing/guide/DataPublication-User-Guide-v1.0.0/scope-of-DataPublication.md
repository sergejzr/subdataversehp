---
title: The scope of DataPublication
---
While text publications and dissertations are published Open Access via the institutional repositories of your home institution, DataPublication is the way to go when looking for a place to publish the research data behind your publication and research project, according to the [FAIR principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/).
