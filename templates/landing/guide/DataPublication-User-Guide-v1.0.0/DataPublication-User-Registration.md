---
title: DataPublication User Registration
---
DataPublication is open to researchers from participating higher-education and research institutions in North Rhine-Westphalia. You can log in with your home institution's account via the federated single sign-on (DFN-AAI/Shibboleth): choose your institution from the list on the login page and authenticate with your usual institutional credentials. Your DataPublication account is created automatically upon first login.

**Please note:** If your institution does not appear in the list, it is not yet connected to DataPublication. In this case, please contact <<DP-SUPPORT-MAIL>>.

Registered DataPublication users can create and edit their datasets. If needed, other users or groups can be granted either read or read-and-write access to the dataset's content.
